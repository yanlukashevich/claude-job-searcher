One card per dig-deeper offer, furthest along first: approved, then complete drafts, then drafts still missing something, contacts only, nothing yet (queue order within each). A card fills up in stages — no contact → contacts (an email, or anything in "other") → draft (subject + body) → approved (you clicked OK to send) — and leaves this list once it has been mailed; history shows the email on the offer's row. Agents can fill the same fields straight in finder/data/dig_deeper.json; this page re-reads the file whenever you come back to the tab. Changing the email, subject, body or CV after approving voids the approval.
